New words:


海: sea, beach


強い: strong, powerful


風: wind, breeze


ふく: to blow (wind)


川 (kawa): river, stream


魚: fish


今日 (kyou): today, this day








夕飯: evening meal


使う: to use, to make use of


料理: cooking, cuisine


となり: neighbor, next to


教室: classroom


歌: song


聞こえる (kikoeru): to be heard, to be audible


まど: window


閉める: to close, to shut
