New words:


店: shop, store


主人: employer, master, host, landlord, one’s husband








いつも: always, every time


着物: clothing, clothes


着る (kiru): to wear (from the shoulders down), to put on


こうちょう: principle, headmaster


かんがえかた: way of thinking


ただしい: right, just, correct, righteous


おもう: to think, to believe, to consider


銀行: bank


以上: more than, not less than, beyond


借りる: to borrow, to rent, to have a loan
