New words:


ドア: door


軽い (karui): light (not heavy)


触る: to touch, to feel


自動的 (jidouteki): automatic


結果 (kekka): result, consequence, outcome


祈る (inoru): to pray, to wish


遠慮 (enryo): diffidence, restraint, reserve


どんどん: rapidly, steadily


いきなり: abruptly, suddenly, all of a sudden


店員 (ten’in): shop assistant, employee, clerk


失神 (shisshin): faint, swoon


たおれる: to fall, to collapse, to go bankrupt


ぐあい: condition, state, manner


じゆう: freedom, liberty


縛る (shibaru): to tie, to bind


気がする (ki ga suru): to have a certain mood or feeling, to have a hunch


心 (kokoro): mind, heart, spirit


たのしい: enjoyable, fun


けいかく: plan, project


水の泡 (mizu no awa): coming to nothing
