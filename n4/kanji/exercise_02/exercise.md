New words:


おもい: heavy


にもつ: luggage, baggage


もつ: to hold, to carry, to possess








えき: station


あるく: to walk


地下鉄: underground train, subway


行く: to go, to proceed


病院: hospital


どのくらい: how long, how far, how much


かかる: to take


ふるい: old (not person), aged, ancient


えいが: movie, film


おんがく: music


すばらしい: wonderful, splendid, magnificent
