New words:


お兄さん: older brother, elder brother


建物: building








出口: exit, gateway, way out


立つ: to stand, to rise, to stand up


茶色: light brown, tawny




服: clothes (esp. Western clothes)


着る: to wear (from the shoulders down)


町: town, block, neighborhood


人口: population


去年: last year


多く: many, much, largely
