New words:


電車 (densha): (electric) train


遅れる: to be late, to be delayed


計算 (keisan): calculation, count, reckoning


ただしい: right, just, correct


答え (kotae): response, reply, answer, solution


もちろん: of course, certainly


合う (au): to come together, to fit, to match


内容 (naiyou): contents, subject


ふくしゅう: review, revision


外国人 (gaikokujin): foreigner


漢字 (kanji): Kanji, Chinese characters


簡単 (kantan): simple


日本人 (nihonjin): Japanese person, Japanese people


お正月 (oshougatsu): New Year, New Year’s Day


物 (mono): thing, object


与える: to give, to bestow


風邪 (kaze): cold (illness), common cold


すっかり: all, completely, thoroughly


なおる: to be cured, to get well, to be healed


ちゃんと: properly, exactly


暗い: dark, gloomy


失礼します (shitsurei shimasu): excuse me


煙草 (tabako): tobacco, cigarettes


吸う: to smoke, to breathe in, to inhale


こうつう: communication, transportation, traffic


便利 (benri): convenient, handy, useful
