New words:


休み (yasumi): vacation, holiday


寂しい: lonely, lonesome, solitary


もしかして: perhaps, possibly


冷える (hieru): to grow cold, to get chilly, to cool down


体 (karada): body, health


温める (atatameru): to warm, to heat


ストーブ: heater (from stove)


ゲーム: game


パソコン: personal computer


画面 (gamen): terminal screen, scene


向かう: to face, to go towards


楽 (raku): comfort, ease


通う (kayou): to go back and forth, to commute, to attend (school, church)


大事: important, valuable


使う (tsukau): to use, to make use of


お喋り (oshaberi): chattering, talk, idle talk, chitchat


教室 (kyoushitsu): classroom


戻る: to return, to go back


暇 (hima): spare time, free time, time off


花見 (hanami): cherry blossom viewing, flower viewing


しょうせつ: novel, short story


生徒 (seito): pupil


退学 (taigaku): dropping out of school


一度 (ichido): once, one time


いけん: opinion, view


徒歩 (toho): walking, going on foot


とうちゃく: arrival


さくぶん: composition, writing


出す (dasu): to turn in, to submit
