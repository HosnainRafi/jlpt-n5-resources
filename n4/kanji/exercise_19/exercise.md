New words:


珍しい: unusual, rare


植物 (shokubutsu): plant, vegetation


集める (atsumeru): to collect, to gather


食事: meal


終わる (owaru): to finish, to end, to close


流し (nagashi): sink


昔 (mukashi): olden days, former


いろんな: various


物語: tale, story, legend


説明書: (printed) instructions


きぶん: feeling, mood


悪い (warui): bad, poor, evil


しんせつ: kindness, gentleness
