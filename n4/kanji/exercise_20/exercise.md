New words:


手紙 (tegami): letter


折る: to fold, to break


封筒 (fuutou): envelope


電話 (denwa): telephone


授業 (jugyou): lesson, class work


ベル: bell


鳴る: to sound, to ring, to echo


喜ぶ: to be delighted, to be glad, to be pleased


パーティー: party


参加 (sanka): participation


工場: factory, plant, mill


機械 (kikai): machine, mechanism


故障 (koshou): break-down, failure


大変 (taihen): very, greatly, immense, difficult, hard, disaster


みなさん: all, everyone, everybody


そだてる: to raise, to bring up


学生 (gakusei): student


一生懸命 (isshoukenmei): very hard, with utmost effort


貿易: trade (foregin)
