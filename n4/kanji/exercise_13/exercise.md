New words:


水着: bathing suit, swimsuit


着る (kiru): to wear (from the shoulders down)


姿 (sugata): figure, shape


写真 (shashin): photo, photograph


撮る (toru): to take (a photo)


はずかしい: shy, ashamed, embarrassed


話 (hanashi): talk, speech, chat, story


試験 (shiken): test, examination


パス: passing (an examination)


お茶 (ocha): tea


こい: strong (flavor, smell), thick, dense


苦い (nigai): bitter


甘い: sweet, delicious


果物 (kudamono): fruit


研究 (kenkyuu): study, research


計画 (keikaku): plan, project, schedule


興味: interest (in something)


ゆうべ: last night, evening


遅く (osoku): late


寝る (neru): to sleep


ねむい: sleepy, drowsy


歌う (utau): to sing


踊る: to dance


靴 (kutsu): shoes, footwear


壊れる: to be broken, to break


げんかん: entranceway, entry hall


気を付ける (ki o tsukeru): take care, be careful


ズボン: trousers


泥: mud


付く (tsuku): to stain, to be attached
