New words:


重要: important, essential


書類 (shorui): documents, official papers


無くす (nakusu): to lose something, to get rid of


忙しい (isogashii): busy, occupied


誘い (sasoi): invitation, temptation


断る: to refuse, to reject, to turn down


皮 (kawa): skin, pelt


実際 (jissai): practicality, reality


ぬの: cloth


荷物 (nimotsu): luggage, baggage


重い (omoi): heavy


専門 (senmon): specialty, expert


会社 (kaisha): company, corporation, workplace


届ける: to deliver, to forward


指輪: (finger) ring


自分 (jibun): myself, yourself, oneself


作る (tsukuru): to make, to produce


しっぱい: failure, mistake, blunder


大丈夫 (daijoubu): okay, all right


頑張る (ganbaru): to try one’s best, to stand firm


適当: adequate, suitable, relevant


普段: usual, habitual, ordinary


使う (tsukau): to use, to make use of


言葉 (kotoba): word, phrase


正午 (shougo): noon, midday


太陽 (taiyou): sun, solar


ひかり: light


特に (toku ni): particularly, especially


知る (shiru): to know, to understand, to be acquainted with


財布 (saifu): wallet


ひろう: to pick up, to find
