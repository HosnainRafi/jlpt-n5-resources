New words:


住む: to live, to reside, to inhibit


町 (machi): town, block, neighborhood


海: sea, beach


近い: near, close, short (distance)


自動車: automobile


工業: (manufacturing) industry








さかん: popular, prosperous, vigorous


封筒 (fuutou): envelope


切手: stamp (postage)


貼る (haru): to stick, to paste, to affix


送る: to send (a thing), to dispatch


きょねん: last year


はる: spring, springtime


そつぎょうしき: graduation ceremony


おわる: to end, to finish
