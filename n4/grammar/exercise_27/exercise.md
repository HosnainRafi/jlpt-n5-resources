New words:


そう言えば (sou ieba): come to think of it, now that you mention it


ベル: bell


鳴る (naru): to sound, to ring


きっと: surely, undoubtedly


図書館 (toshokan): library


鳥 (tori): bird








大変 (taihen): very, greatly


優しい (yasashii): kind, gentle


忘れる (wasureru): to forget


切符 (kippu): ticket


他 (hoka): other, the rest


手紙 (tegami): letter


気を悪くする (ki o waruku suru): to take offense at, to to feel hurt by


祖父 (sofu): grandfather


アル中 (aruchuu): alcoholism


ベルト: belt


殴る (naguru): to strike, to hit
