New words:


どうしても: by all means, at any cost


窓 (mado): window


外 (soto): outside, exterior


雨 (ame): rain


降る (furu): to fall


気に入る (ki ni iru): to be pleased with, to suit


楽しい (tanoshii): enjoyable, fun


生まれる (umareru): to be born








子牛 (koushi): calf


立ち上がる (tachiagaru): to stand up, to get up


何か (nanika): something


働く (hataraku): to work, to labor


易しい (yasashii): easy, simple


料理 (ryouri): cooking, cuisine


習う (narau): to learn


中国 (chuugoku): China


旅する (tabisuru): to travel


実家 (jikka): (one’s parents’) home


連絡 (renraku): contacting, getting in touch




機械 (kikai): machine, mechanism


工作 (kousaku): work, construction


凄い (sugoi): amazing, great


汚す (yogosu): to make dirty, to stain


今朝 (kesa): this morning


早く (hayaku): early, quickly


学校 (gakkou): school
