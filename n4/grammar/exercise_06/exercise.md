New words:


いろいろ: various


調べる (shiraberu): to examine, to investigate, to check up


たぶん: perhaps, probably


声 (koe): voice


休む (yasumu): to take a day off, to rest, to have a break


思う (omou): to think, to consider, to believe


レポート: report


先輩 (senpai): senior (at work or school), superior


お土産 (omiyage): present, souvenir


部屋 (heya): room, chamber


広い (hiroi): spacious, vast, wide
