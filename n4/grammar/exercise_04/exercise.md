New words:


楽しい (tanoshii): enjoyable, fun, pleasant, happy


顔 (kao): face, look, expression


素敵 (suteki): lovely, beautiful, great, superb


ごほうび: reward, prize


切り抜き (kirinuki): clipping (of newspaper article, etc.), cutting, cut-out


テニス: tennis


行う (okonau): to be done, to take place, to be held


苦労 (kurou): troubles, hardships


息子 (musuko): son


大学 (daigaku): university, college


卒業 (sotsugyou): graduation, completion (e.g. of a course)


決める (kimeru): to decide, to choose, to determine


成功 (seikou): success, hit


手がける (tegakeru): to handle, to manage, to deal with, to work with


今朝 (kesa): this morning


目覚める (mezameru): to wake up, to awake, to awaken to


昨夜 (sakuya/yuube): last night, yesterday evening


思い出す (omoidasu): to recall, to remember, to recollect


少し (sukoshi): little, few


時間がかかる (jikan ga kakaru): to take time
