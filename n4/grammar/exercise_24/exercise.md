New words:


バー: bar


ビール: beer


ゲーム: game


気にする (ki ni suru): to mind, to care about


辺り (atari): vicinity, neighborhood


バッテリー: battery


レーダー: radar


かっこいい: attractive, good-looking, cool


仕事 (shigoto): work, job


実は (jitsu wa): as a matter of fact, to tell you the truth


秘密 (himitsu): secret


大事 (daiji): important, valuable


壊す (kowasu): to break, to destroy


叱られる (shikarareru): to be scolded

掛ける (kakeru): to hang, to sit


少し (sukoshi): little, few

説明 (setsumei): explanation


泊まる (tomaru): to stay at (a hotel)


お金 (okane): money


正直 (shoujiki): honestly, frankly
