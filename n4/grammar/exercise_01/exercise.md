New words:


病気 (byouki): illness, disease


薬 (kusuri): medicine


沢山 (takusan): many, a lot


飲む (nomu): to drink, to swallow


週間 (shuukan): week, weekly


戻る (modoru): to return, to go back


思う (omou): to think, to consider








決める (kimeru): to decide


社長 (shachou): company president


出かける (dekakeru): to depart, to go out


準備 (junbi): preparation, setup


部屋 (heya): room


閉める (shimeru): to close, to shut


郵便局 (yuubinkyoku): post office


通る (tooru): to pass by, to go through


守る (mamoru): to protect, to keep


不安 (fuan): anxiety, uneasiness


命 (inochi): (mortal) life


夕方 (yuugata): evening
