New words:


上手 (jouzu): skill, skillful, dexterity


留学 (ryuugaku): studying abroad usually at university level)


富士山 (fujisan): Mount Fuji


のぼる: to climb, to go up, to ascend


空 (sora): sky, the air


来月 (raigetsu): next month


知らせる (shiraseru): to notify, to advise, to inform


結婚 (kekkon): marriage


さっき: some time ago, just now


起きる (okiru): to get up, to rise


眠い (nemui): sleepy, drowsy
