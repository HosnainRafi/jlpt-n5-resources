New words:


バケツ: bucket


一杯 (ippai): full, to the utmost


帰る (kaeru): to return, to come home


近く (chikaku): near, nearly


食事 (shokuji): meal


果物 (kudamono): fruit


野菜 (yasai): vegetable








経つ (tatsu): to pass, to lapse


彼女 (kanojo): she, her


返事 (henji): reply, answer


階 (kai): stories


音 (oto): sound, noise


誰も (daremo): anyone, no-one


気づく (kizuku): to notice, to recognize


半分 (hanbun): half


眠る (nemuru): to sleep


動き回る (ugokimawaru): to move around


喋る (shaberu): to talk, to chat


楽しむ (tanoshimu): to enjoy oneself


彼 (kare): he, him


叫ぶ (sakebu): to shout, to cry


違う (chigau): to differ, to vary


最近 (saikin): nowadays, most recent
