New words:


約束 (yakusoku): promise, one’s word, date, engagement


忘れる (wasureru): to forget, to leave carelessly


昼ごはん (hirugohan): lunch, midday meal


暇 (hima): spare time, free time, leisure


風景 (fuukei): scenery, scene, landscape


ゆっくり: slowly, at ease, restful


通り過ぎる (toorisugiru): to pass, to pass through


歩き回る (arukimawaru): to walk about, to walk to and fro, to pace around


心配 (shinpai): worry, concern, anxiety


気分が悪い (kibun ga warui): feel sick, feel unwell


手作り (tezukuri): handmade, homegrown, homemade


チョコ: chocolate


便り (tayori): news, tidings, correspondence, letter


彼女 (kanojo): she, her


得意 (tokui): one’s strong point, one’s forte, one’s specialty
