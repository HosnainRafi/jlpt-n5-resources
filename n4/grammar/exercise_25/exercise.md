New words:


将来 (shourai): future


医者 (isha): (medical) doctor


スナック: snack


一緒に (issho ni): together with


食堂 (shokudou): dining room, cafeteria


夕食 (yuushoku): evening meal, dinner


話しかける (hanashikakeru): to talk to someone


ちゃんと: properly, exactly








プール: pool


泳ぐ (oyogu): to swim


作る (tsukuru): to make, to produce


戸棚 (todana): cupboard, locker


大学 (daigaku): university, college


進む (susumu): to advance, to go forward


事件 (jiken): event, affair, incident


頭がいい (atama ga ii): bright, intelligent
