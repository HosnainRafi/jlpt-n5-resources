New words:


靴 (kutsu): shoes, footwear


履く (haku): to put on (or wear) lower-body clothing


顔 (kao): face (person)


合う (au): to fit, to match


似る (niru): to resemble, to be similar


伝える (tsutaeru): to convey, to report


言葉 (kotoba): word, phrase


不便 (fuben): inconvenience


丁寧 (teinei): polite, courteous


荷物 (nimotsu): luggage, baggage


急ぐ (isogu): to hurry, to rush








うっかり: carelessly, thoughtlessly


ゆっくり: slowly, at ease


迎える (mukaeru): to go out to meet, to welcome


お湯 (oyu): hot water


沸く (waku): to boil, to grow hot


焼ける (yakeru): to burn, to be roasted
