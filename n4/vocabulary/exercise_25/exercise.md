New words:


仕事 (shigoto): work, job


多い (ooi): many, numerous


遊ぶ (asobu): to play, to enjoy oneself


働く (hataraku): to work


休む (yasumu): to rest


努める (tsutomeru): to endeavor, to try


本屋 (honya): bookstore, bookshop


住む (sumu): to live, to reside


もっと: more, even more


きっと: surely, undoubtedly


ずっと: the whole time, all along


湖 (mizuumi): lake


深い (fukai): deep, profound


寒い (samui): cold


重い (omoi): heavy








病院 (byouin): hospital

警官 (keikan): policeman

ぐっすり: sound asleep, fast asleep


はっきり: clearly, plainly


苦い (nigai): bitter
