New words:


課長 (kachou): section manager, section chief


熱 (netsu): fever, temperature


命 (inochi): (mortal) life


助かる (tasukaru): to be saved, to be rescued


有能 (yuunou): able, capable


きっと: surely, undoubtedly


親切 (shinsetsu): kindness, gentleness


感謝 (kansha): thanks, gratitude


桜 (sakura): cherry tree, cherry blossom


種類 (shurui): variety, kind, type


種 (shu): kind, variety


女性 (josei): woman, female


管理職 (kanrishoku): management


割 (wari): rate, ratio, proportion


程度 (teido): degree, amount


丁寧 (teinei): polite, courteous


商品 (shouhin): commodity, goods, merchandise


成立 (seiritsu): coming into existence, establishment


品物 (shinamono): goods, article, thing


買い手 (kaite): buyer
