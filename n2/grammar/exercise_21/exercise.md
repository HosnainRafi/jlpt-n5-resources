New words:


真実 (shinjitsu): truth, reality


死者 (shisha): casualty, deceased


帯出 (taishutsu): taking out, using at home


持ち出す (mochidasu): to take out, to carry out


火事 (kaji): fire, conflagration


自動的 (jidouteki): automatic


途方もない (tohou mo nai): extraordinary, outrageous, absurd


人殺し (hitogoroshi): murder, murderer


泥棒 (dorobou): thief, burglar, robber


親しい (shitashii): close, intimate


仲間 (nakama): company, colleague, fellow


社長 (shachou): company president


アイデア: idea


気に入る (ki ni iru): to be pleased with, to suit


キーパッド: keypad


打ち込む (uchikomu): to drive in, to shoot into


ブザー: buzzer


生徒 (seito): pupil


立ち止まる (tachidomaru): to stop, to halt
