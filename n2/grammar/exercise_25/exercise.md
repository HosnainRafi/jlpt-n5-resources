New words:


敵に回す (teki ni mawasu): to make an enemy (of)


思い出す (omoidasu): to recall, to remember


踏み込む (fumikomu): to step into, to break into


移動 (idou): removal, migration, movement


逆戻り (gyakumodori): reversal, relapse


追っ手 (otte): pursuer, pursuing party


バス停 (basutei): bus stop


出発時刻 (shuppatsu jikoku): departure time, flight time


満員 (man’in): full house, no vacancy, sold out


結局 (kekkyoku): eventually, in the end


事業計画 (jigyou keikaku): business plan, business schedule


投資 (toushi): investment


アドバイス: advice


タダ: free of charge


戦争 (sensou): war


政治 (seiji): politics, government


さっぱり: not at all, completely ignorant


感心 (kanshin): admiration


扱う (atsukau): to handle, to deal with


関わる (kakawaru): to be concerned with, to have to do with


後ろめたい (ushirometai): feeling guilty, with a guilty conscience


緊張 (kinchou): tension, nervousness


監督 (kantoku): coach, manager, director


決勝戦 (kesshousen): finals game of a tournament, decision of a contest


世間 (seken): world, society
