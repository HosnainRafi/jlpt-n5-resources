New words:


美貌 (bibou): beautiful face, good looks


惑わす (madowasu): to bewilder, to perplex, to puzzle


敵 (teki): opponent, rival, adversary


安定 (antei): stability, equilibrium


危うい (ayaui): dangerous, risky, hazardous


苦労 (kurou): troubles, hardships


物事 (monogoto): things, everything


悩む (nayamu): to be worried, to be troubled


返事 (henji): reply, answer, response


諦める (akirameru): to give up, to abandon


取りやめ (toriyame): cancellation, suspension


落ち合う (ochiau): to meet, to rendezvous


生気 (seiki): life, vitality, vigor


覗き込む (nozokikomu): to look into, to peer in


自宅 (jitaku): one’s home


読書 (dokusho): reading


目覚める (mezameru): to wake up


想像 (souzou): imagination, guess


愛し合う (aishiau): to love one another


洗面台 (senmendai): washbasin, washstand


鏡 (kagami): mirror


病室 (byoushitsu): sickroom, hospital room


映る (utsuru): to be reflected


対面 (taimen): interview, meeting
