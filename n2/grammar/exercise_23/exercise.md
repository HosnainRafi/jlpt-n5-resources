New words:


長い間 (nagai aida): long interval


明かす (akasu): to reveal, to pass (the night)


試す (tamesu): to test, to try out


驚かす (odorokasu): to surprise, to frighten


マラソン: marathon


大会 (taikai): convention, tournament


出場 (shutsujou): participation, appearance


自分自身 (jibunjishin): oneself, myself


貴い (tattoi/toutoi): precious, valuable, priceless


過去 (kako): the past, bygone days


罪 (tsumi): crime, sin


赦す (yurusu): to forgive, to let off


条件 (jouken): condition, terms


屋敷 (yashiki): residence, estate, premises


確か (tashika): certain, sure, definite


建築 (kenchiku): construction, architecture


建てる (tateru): to build, to construct


入用 (nyuuyou): need, requirement, demand


建て増し (tatemashi): building addition or extension


制御 (seigyo): control, governing, suppression


状態 (joutai): current status


事件 (jiken): event, affair, incident


恐怖 (kyoufu): fear, dread, dismay


与える (ataeru): to give, to offer
