New words:


目標 (mokuhyou): mark, objective, target


競争 (kyousou): competition, contest


打ち勝つ (uchikatsu): to conquer, to overcome


儲ける (moukeru): to get, to earn, to gain


製品 (seihin): manufactured goods, product


すさまじい: terrific, fierce, dreadful


そっぽを向く (soppo o muku): to turn away, to face away


スケジュール: schedule


詰まる (tsumaru): to be blocked


問い (toi): question, query


否定的 (hiteiteki): negative, contradictory


業界 (gyoukai): industry, business


数年 (suunen): several years


波 (nami): wave


腕 (ude): arm, skill


突っ伏す (tsuppusu): to fall prostrate


責任 (sekinin): duty, responsibility


高飛車 (takabisha): on one’s high horse, domineering


言葉遣い (kotobazukai): speech, expression, wording


数々 (kazukazu): many


なだれる: to slope, to descend
