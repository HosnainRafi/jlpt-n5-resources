New words:


送別会 (soubetsukai): farewell party


歓迎会 (kangeikai): welcome party


夜遅く (yoruosoku): late at night, at a late hour


無口 (mukuchi): reticence, taciturnity


超然 (chouzen): detached, aloof, transcendental


家出 (iede): running away from home, leaving home


聡明 (soumei): wisdom, sagacity


技術 (gijutsu): art, craft, technique


進歩 (shinpo): progress, development


気が散る (ki ga chiru): to get distracted, to jump off the track


成長率 (seichouritsu): growth rate


資産価値 (shisan kachi): value of one’s property (assets), property value


ふくらむ: to expand, to swell, to become inflated


スライド: slide


本社 (honsha): head office, main office, headquarters


デザイン: design
