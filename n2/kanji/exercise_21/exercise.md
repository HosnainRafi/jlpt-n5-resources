New words:


食料 (shokuryou): food


ほぞん: preservation, conservation, storage


しゅだん: means, way, measure


塩 (shio): salt


かんづめ: packing (in cans), tin can


かんそう: dry, dehydrated


方法 (houhou): method, way, means


強盗: robber, robbery, burglary


鋭い: pointed, sharp


腕: arm, skill


刺す (sasu): to pierce, to stab, to thrust


ガラス: glass


はへん: fragment, broken piece


指 (yubi): finger, toe


けが: injury, hurt


包帯 (houtai): bandage


巻く (maku): to coil, to roll
