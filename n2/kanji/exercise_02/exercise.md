New words:


放送局: broadcasting office


正面: front, frontage, facade


駐車 (chuusha): parking (e.g. car)


停車: stopping (e.g. train)


禁止: prohibition, ban, inhibition


男女 (danjo): man and woman, men and women


平等 (byoudou): equality, impartiality


テーマ: topic, theme


会議 (kaigi): meeting, conference


各国: each nation, many countries


代表: representative, delegation


出席: attendance, presence


家庭: home, family, household


職場: one’s post, place of work, workplace
