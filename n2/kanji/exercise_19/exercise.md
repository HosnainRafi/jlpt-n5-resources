New words:


印刷: printing


技術: art, craft, technique


比べる: to compare, to make a comparison


進歩: progress, development


さい: -years-old


そふ: grandfather


住宅 (juutaku): residence, housing, residential building


光: light


奥: interior, inner part, inside


届く: to reach, to arrive, to get through, to be delivered
