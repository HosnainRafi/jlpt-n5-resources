New words:


つい: just (now); quite (near); unintentionally; unconsciously


ばったり: with a clash; with a thud; with a bang; suddenly (ending); abruptly (coming to a halt)


幸い (saiwai): happiness; blessedness; luck; fortune; felicity​


用心 (youjin): care; precaution; guarding; caution


無事 (buji): safety; peace; quietness


不足 (fusoku): insufficiency; shortage; deficiency


伝える (tsutaeru): to convey; to report; to transmit


配る (kubaru): to distribute; to deliver


気が長い (ki ga nagai): patient; having a long fuse


国内 (kokunai): internal; domestic


可能 (kanou): potential; practicable; possible


海外 (kaigai): foreign; abroad; overseas


通知 (tsuuchi): notice; notification; report


通信 (tsuushin): correspondence; communication; transmission


通行 (tsuukou): passage; passing; common usage


通用 (tsuuyou): popular use; circulation; to pass as; to pass for


あたりまえ: natural; reasonable; obvious


なまいき: impertinent; saucy; cheeky; conceit


おおざっぱ: rough (as in not precise); broad; sketchy


えらそう: self-important; important-looking; proud


活動 (katsudou): action; activity


認める (mitomeru): to recognize; to approve; to deem acceptable


自慢 (jiman): pride; boast


努力 (doryoku): great effort; exertion; endeavor


目的 (mokuteki): purpose; goal; aim


覚悟 (kakugo): resolution; resignation; readiness


真の (shin no): true; real; genuine


国際交流 (kokusai kouryuu): international exchange


期間 (kikan): period; term; interval


期限 (kigen): term; deadline; time limit


時期 (jiki): time; season; period; phase


米 (kome): rice


収穫 (shuukaku): harvest; crop; results


あるいは: or; possibly


したがって: therefore; consequently


ただし: but; however


ところで: by the way; incidentally


なんでも: any; whatever one likes


なんだか: somewhat; somehow


なんとも: quite; really; not a bit; not at all
