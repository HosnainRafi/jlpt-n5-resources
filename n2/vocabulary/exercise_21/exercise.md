New words:

安価 (anka): low price; cheapness


しばらく: little while; short while


下げる (sageru): to hang; to lower

理解 (rikai): understanding; comprehension; appreciation


暗記 (anki): memorization; learning by heart


感動 (kandou): being deeply moved emotionally; excitement

くだらない: good-for-nothing; stupid; worthless; trivial


ずうずうしい: impudent; shameless


騙す (damasu): to trick; to cheat; to deceive
