New words:


なんとなく: somehow or other; for some reason or another


なんとか: something; something or other; one way or another


なんでも: any; whatever one likes; by all means


変更 (henkou): change; modification; alteration


提案 (teian): proposal; proposition; suggestion


展開 (tenkai): development; evolution; progressing


観察 (kansatsu): observation; survey; watching


投票 (touhyou): voting; poll


書類 (shorui): documents; official papers


テーマ: theme; topic


シーツ: sheet; bad sheet


コピー: copy; photocopy


文章 (bunshou): sentence; article; composition


まとめる: to collect; to put (it all) together


整理 (seiri): sorting; arrangement; putting in order


要旨 (youshi): point; essentials; gist; summary


要領 (youryou): point; gist; essentials; outline


台風 (taifuu): typhoon; hurricane


備える (sonaeru): to furnish; to equip; to provide for


いきいき: vividly; lively


いろいろ: various


いよいよ: at last; finally


いちいち: one-by-one; separately; every single


せいぜい: at the most; at best; to the utmost


しみじみ: earnestly; keenly; deeply


ちかぢか: soon; nearness; before long


ひろびろ: extensive; spacious


蓄える (takuwaeru): to store; to save up; to lay in stock


雇う (yatou): to employ; to hire


捕える (toraeru): to catch; to capture; to arrest


懐かしい (natsukashii): dear; desired; missed


詳しい (kuwashii): detailed; full; accurate


大人しい (otonashii): obedient; docile; quiet


騒がしい (sawagashii): noisy


家具屋 (kaguya): furniture store; furniture dealer


発達 (hattatsu): development; growth


配達 (haitatsu): delivery; distribution


速達 (sokutatsu): express; special delivery


伝達 (dentatsu): transmission; communication
