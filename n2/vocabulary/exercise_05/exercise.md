New words:

企業 (kigyou): enterprise; undertaking; corporation


給料 (kyuuryou): salary; wages


異なる (kotonaru): to differ; to vary


基準 (kijun): standard; basis; criterion


標準 (hyoujun): standard; level; norm


定価 (teika): list price; established price


平均 (heikin): average; mean; balance


ジョギング: jogging


特定 (tokutei): specific; particular; designated; special


統一 (touitsu): unity; uniformity; unification


専念 (sennen): absorption; giving undivided attention; devoting oneself (to)


注目 (chuumoku): notice; attention; observation


作品 (sakuhin): work (e.g. book, film, composition, etc.)
