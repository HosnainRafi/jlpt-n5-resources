New words:


大都市 (daitoshi): metropolis; large city


交通 (koutsuu): traffic; transportation; communication


住宅 (juutaku): residence; housing; residential building


様々 (samazama): varied; various


抱える (kakaeru): to have (esp. problems, debts, etc.)


寄付金 (kifukin): donation; contribution, endowment


退屈 (taikutsu): tedium; boredom


無事 (buji): safety; peace; quietness


有効 (yuukou): valid; effective


粗末 (somatsu): crude; rough; plain


検討 (kentou): consideration; examination; investigation


備える (sonaeru): to offer; to sacrifice; to dedicate


揃える (soroeru): to collect; to gather; to get together


蓄える (takuwaeru): to store; to save up; to lay in stock


例える (tatoeru): to compare (something) to; to liken; to use a metaphor


精神 (seishin): mind; soul; heart; spirit


性格 (seikaku): character; personality


心理 (shinri): state of mind; mentality


感情 (kanjou): emotion; feeling; sentiment


場面 (bamen): scene; setting (e.g. of novel)


結構 (kekkou): splendid; nice; wonderful


環境 (kankyou): environment; circumstance


自然 (shizen): nature; natural; spontaneous


格別 (kakubetsu): particular; special; exceptional


過剰 (kajou): excess; surplus; over-


巨大 (kyodai): huge; gigantic; enormous


強引 (gouin): overbearing; coercive; pushy; forcible


有能 (yuunou): able; capable; efficient


欠陥 (kekkan): defect; fault; deficiency


限界 (genkai): limit; bound


欠点 (ketten): fault; defect; flaw; weak point


無限 (mugen): infinity; infinitude


いまにも: at any time; soon
