New words:


カロリー: calorie


ナンバー: number


アイデア: idea


詳しい (kuwashii): detailed; full; accurate


さて: well; now; then


ただ: ordinary; common; usual


しかも: moreover; furthermore


または: or; otherwise


真っ先に (massaki ni): at the very beginning; first of all


真っ白 (masshiro): pure white


引き受ける (hikiukeru): to take on; to undertake; to take charge of


役立つ (yakudatsu): to be useful; to be helpful


任せる (makaseru): to entrust (e.g. a task) to another; to leave to


テープレコーダー: tape recorder


電池 (denchi): battery; cell


完成 (kansei): complete; completion; perfection


対立 (tairitsu): confrontation; opposition


やむを得ない (yamu o enai): cannot be helped; unavoidable


信号 (shingou): traffic light


いっそう: much more; still more


いっぱんに: in general; generally


いっせいに: simultaneously; all at once


いったん: once; for a short time


文学賞 (bungakushou): literary prize


作家 (sakka): author; writer; novelist


新聞記者 (shinbun kisha): newspaper reporter


ステージ: stage; performance


コンクール: competition; contest


インタビュー: interview


レクリエーション: recreation


水泳 (suiei): swimming


混ざる (mazaru): to be mixed; to be blended with
