New words:


顔が広い (kao ga hiroi): having a large, diverse circle of acquaintances​


幼稚園 (youchien): kindergarten


向き (muki): direction; tendency; inclination


向かい (mukai): facing; opposite; across the street


向かう (mukau): to face; to go towards; to head towards


向け (muke): intended for; oriented towards; aimed at


予算 (yosan): estimate; budget


中止 (chuushi): suspension; stoppage; discontinuance


削る (kezuru): to cut down (budget, expenses, salary, etc.)


用事 (youji): tasks; things to do; errand


預かる (azukaru): to look after; to take care of; to keep in custody


預ける (azukeru): to give into custody; to leave (a child) in the care of; to entrust


与える (ataeru): to give; to bestow; to grant


新鮮 (shinsen): fresh


正確 (seikaku): accurate; punctual; exact


透明 (toumei): transparent; clear


明確 (meikaku): clear; precise; definite


憎らしい (nikurashii): odious; hateful; detestable


恐ろしい (osoroshii): terrible; dreadful; terrifying


大人しい (otonashii): obedient; docile; quiet


やかましい: noisy; loud


満足 (manzoku): satisfaction; contentment


サービス: service; help


リズム: rhythm


感心 (kanshin): admiration; being impressed


意志 (ishi): will; volition; intent; determination


感情 (kanjou): emotion; feeling; sentiment


偏る (katayoru): to lean (to one side); to incline; to be biased


繋がる (tsunagaru): to be tied together; to be connected to


流行る (hayaru): to be popular; to come into fashion; to spread widely (e.g. disease)


雇う (yatou): to employ; to hire


普通 (futsuu): general; ordinary; usual


平凡 (heibon): common; commonplace; ordinary


普段 (fudan): usual; habitual; ordinary


本来 (honrai): originally; primarily; essentially
