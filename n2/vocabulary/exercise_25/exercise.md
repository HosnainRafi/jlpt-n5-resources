New words:


気楽 (kiraku): carefree; comfortable; at ease; easygoing


けんきょ: modesty; humility


粗末 (somatsu): crude; rough; plain; humble


手ごろ (tegoro): moderate; handy; convenient; reasonable


熱心 (nesshin): zeal; enthusiasm


集中 (shuuchuu): concentration


上達 (joutatsu): improvement (e.g. in skill, ability); advance


専門 (senmon): speciality; specialty; subject of study; area of expertise


活気 (kakki): energy; liveliness


全身 (zenshin): whole (body); full-length


全体 (zentai): whole; entirety


全力 (zenryoku): all one’s power; one’s utmost


全集 (zenshuu): complete works


同僚 (douryou): coworker; colleague; associate


恐れる (osoreru): to fear; to be afraid of


競争 (kyousou): competition; contest; to compete


健闘 (kentou): fighting bravely; strenuous efforts


摩擦 (masatsu): friction; rubbing; rubdown


損害 (songai): damage; injury; loss


就職 (shuushoku): finding employment; getting a job


率 (ritsu): rate; ratio


割 (wari): rate; ratio; proportion


畑 (hatake): field; cultivated land


見出し (midashi): heading; headline; title


見かけ (mikake): outward appearance; apparent


見直し (minaoshi): review; reconsideration; revision


見方 (mikata): viewpoint; point of view


嗅ぐ (kagu): to sniff; to smell


振り向く (furimuku): to turn one’s face; to turn around


付き合う (tsukiau): to associate with; to keep company with


当てはめる (atehameru): to apply; to adapt


近づける (chikazukeru): to bring near; to let come near


温める (atatameru): to warm; to heat


冷める (sameru): to become cool; to cool down


薄める (usumeru): to dilute; to water down


弱める (yowameru): to weaken


監督 (kantoku): supervision; control; director; supervisor


管理 (kanri): control; management


生産 (seisan): production; manufacture


調節 (chousetsu): regulation; adjustment; control
