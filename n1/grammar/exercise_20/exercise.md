New words:


プランテーション: plantation


女主人 (onnnashujin): mistress (of the house), hostess, landlady


引き受ける (hikiukeru): to undertake, to take up, to be responsible for


心構え (kokorogamae): preparedness, readiness


身長 (shinchou): height (of body), stature


鉄製 (tessei): made from iron


レール: rail


交換 (koukan): exchange, interchange, substitution


あらゆる: all, every


補充 (hojuu): supplementation, replenishment


困難 (konnan): difficulty, distress


大げさに言う (oogesa ni iu): to exaggerate


いたずら: tease, prank, mischief


裁判所 (saibansho): court, courthouse


スキャンダラス: scandalous


前線 (zensen): front line, (weather) front


兵力 (heiryoku): military force, force of arms


衰える (otoroeru): to become weak, to decline


後方 (kouhou): behind, in the rear, in back


毒物 (dokubutsu): poison


熟練工 (jukurenkou): skilled workman, master mechanic


連合軍 (rengougun): the Allies, allied forces


大砲 (taihou): gun, cannon, artillery


弾薬 (dan’yaku): ammunition


難儀 (nangi): hardship, difficulty


天才 (tensai): genius, prodigy


傑作 (kessaku): masterpiece, best work
