New words:


カエル: frog


ともなく: phrase used to express the uncertainty or lack of intent in what precedes it


景気 (keiki): business conditions; economic activity; economic climate


回復 (kaifuku): restoration; recovery


傾向 (keikou): tendency; trend; inclination


大企業 (daikigyou): large company; enterprise


給与 (kyuuyo): pay; salary; wages


支給額 (shikyuugaku): allowance


見込む (mikomu): to anticipate; to estimate; to expect


演奏 (ensou): musical performance


会場 (kaijou): assembly hall


わきあがる： to boil up; to break out; to be in uproar


拍手 (hakushu): clapping hands; applause


割れる (wareru): to break; to be smashed


釈明 (shakumei): explanation


魅力 (miryoku): charm; fasciation; appeal


新商品 (shinshouhin): new product


開発 (kaihatsu): development


特価 (tokka): special price; bargain price


提供 (teikyou): offer


締め切る (shimekiru): to cut off (e.g. because a deadline has expired)


発作 (hossa): fit; spasm; seizure; attack


半日 (hannichi): half day


ひとたび: once; on one occasion


連休 (renkyuu): cosecutive holidays


あふれる: to overflow; to flood
