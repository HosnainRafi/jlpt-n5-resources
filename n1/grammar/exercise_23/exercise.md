New words:


歴史的 (rekishiteki): historic, historical


提案 (teian): proposal, proposition, suggestion


記憶 (kioku): memory, recollection, remembrance


真実 (shinjitsu): truth, reality


断言 (dangen): assertion, declaration, affirmation


本能 (honnou): instinct


おもむく: to go, to proceed, to become


英雄的 (eiyuuteki): heroic


威厳 (igen): dignity, majesty, solemnity


尊崇 (sonsuu): reverence, veneration


夢想 (musou): dream, vision


沈む (shizumu): to sink, to feel depressed


眼瞼 (ganken): eyelid


次第に (shidai ni): gradually


暴れる (abareru): to act violently, to rage


賭け (kake): betting, gambling


不愉快 (fuyukai): discomfort, unpleasantness


見学者 (kengakusha): visitor (to factory, lab, etc.)


デスマスク: death mask


嫌悪 (ken’o): disgust, hate, repugnance


買い取り (kaitori): purchase, sale (transaction)
