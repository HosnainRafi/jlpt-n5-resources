New words:


妊娠 (ninshin): conception, pregnancy


考え直す (kangaenaosu): to reconsider, to rethink


うんざり: tedious, boring


歪める (yugameru): to bend, to curve, to distort


企業 (kigyou): enterprise, undertaking, corporation


対応 (taiou): coping with, dealing with


要素 (youso): component, factor


同席 (douseki): presence, sitting with, being with


功を奏する (kou o sousuru): to succeed, to bear fruit


連合国 (rengoukoku): allied nations, the Allies


乗り込む (norikomu): to board, to embark on


妬む (netamu): to be jealous, to be envious


信ずる (shinzuru): to believe, to believe in
