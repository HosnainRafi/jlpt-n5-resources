New words:


来訪 (raihou): visit, call


告げる (tsugeru): to inform


馬車 (basha): coach, carriage, wagon


預かり所 (azukarisho): cloakroom, parcel room


預ける (azukeru): to give into custody


厳命 (genmei): strict order, peremptory command


スマートフォン: smart phone


需品 (juhin): supplies, quartermaster


合格 (goukaku): passing (exam)


ベスト: best


服装 (fukusou): garments


だいぶ: considerably, greatly, a lot


行き着く (ikitsuku): to arrive at, to end up


安定した (antei shita): steady, stable, firm


駆け上がる (kakeagaru): to run up


感謝の念 (kansha no nen): feelings of gratitude
