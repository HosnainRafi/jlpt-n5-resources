New words:


変わり様 (kawariyou): change, alteration, modification


強風 (kyoufuu): strong wind


退学 (taigaku): dropping out of school


損失 (sonshitsu): loss (e.g. assets or profits)


最小限度 (saishougendo): minimum


休まる (yasumaru): to be rested, to feel at ease


食い止める (kuitomeru): to check, to hold back


脱ぎ捨てる (nugisuteru): to fling, to throw off (clothes)


鮮やか (azayaka): vivid, clear, brilliant


重なる (kasanaru): to be piled up, lie on top of one another


小道 (komichi): path, lane


白熱 (hakunetsu): white heat, climax


ウェディングドレス: wedding dress


訴える (uttaeru): to sue (a person), to appeal to, to complain


申請書 (shinseisho): written application


提出 (teishutsu): to present, to submit, to hand in


締め切り (shimekiri): closing, cut-off, deadline


早めに (hayame ni): ahead of time
