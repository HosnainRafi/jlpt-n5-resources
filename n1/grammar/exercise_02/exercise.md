New words:


惜しむ (oshimu): to hold dear; to regret (e.g. a loss)


鑑賞 (kanshou): appreciation (e.g. of art)


作品 (sakuhin): work (e.g. book, film, composition, etc.)


いっさい: absolutely not (with negative)


手作業 (tesagyou): manual work; manual procedures


要因 (youin): main cause, primary factor


影響 (eikyou): influence; effect


政策 (seisaku): political measures; policy


自ら (mizukara): oneself


税金 (zeikin): tax; duty


国民 (kokumin): people; citizen


義務 (gimu): duty; obligation


節税 (setsuzei): tax reduction; tax avoidance


誰しも (dareshimo): everyone; anyone


ミサイル: missile


多数 (tasuu): large number; many


配置 (haichi): deployment; stationing


防衛 (bouei suru): protection


店先 (misesaki): storefront; shopfront


殺到 (sattou): rush; flood


悪化 (akka): deterioration; getting worse


公衆トイレ (koushuu toire): public restroom


幼稚園 (youchien): kindergarten
