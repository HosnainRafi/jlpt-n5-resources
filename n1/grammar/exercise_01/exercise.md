New words:


害する (gaisuru): to injure, to damage


欲する (hossuru): to want, to desire


一流 (ichiryuu): first-class


出演料 (shutsuenryou): performance (actor’s, singer’s) fee


身を起こす (mi o okosu): to get up


人前で (hitomae de): in public, in company


演技 (engi): acting, performance


デジタルハブ: digital hub


わが社 (wagasha): our company, my company


放っておく (houtte oku): to leave alone, to leave as is, to ignore, to neglect


手遅れ (teokure): being too late, belated treatment


ウォッカ: vodka


飲み干す (nomihosu): to drink up, to drain (cup)
