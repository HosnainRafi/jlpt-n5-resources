New words:


当局 (toukyoku): authorities; authorities concerned


柔軟 (juunan): flexible; soft


姿勢 (shisei): attitude; approach; posture


窓口 (madoguchi): ticket window; counter


高圧的 (kouatsuteki): high-handed; oppressive


対応 (taiou): interaction; correspondence; dealing with; response


市民 (shimin): citizen; public


反発 (hanpatsu): opposition; rebellion


指摘 (shiteki): pointing out


恥をかく (haji o kaku): to be embarrassed; to lose face


普段 (fudan): usual; habitual


センサー: sensor


感知 (kanchi): sensing; noticing; perception


警報 (keihou): alarm; warning


立ち読み (tachiyomi): reading while standing (in a bookstore, etc.)


やすらか: peaceful; calm; restful


最期 (saigo): one’s last moment; one’s death; one’s end


苦労 (kurou): troubles; hardships


失敗 (shippai): failure; mistake; blunder
