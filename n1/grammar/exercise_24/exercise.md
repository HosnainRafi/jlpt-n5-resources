New words:


勘違い (kanchigai): misunderstanding, wrong guess


指摘 (shiteki): pointing out, identification


豪華 (gouka): gorgeous, splendor, extravagance


味わう (ajiwau): to taste, to savor


宣言 (sengen): declaration, proclamation


誓い (chikai): oath, vow


生命 (seimei): life, existence


賭ける (kakeru): to wager, to bet, to gamble


プライバシー: privacy


漏らす (morasu): to let leak, to reveal


物足りない (monotarinai): unsatisfied, unsatisfactory


貧乏人 (binbounin): poor man, the poor


一両日 (ichiryoujitsu): a day or two


継続 (keizoku): continuation


管理 (kanri): control, management (of a business)


焦点 (shouten): focus, focal point


転向 (tenkou): conversion, shift, switch


詰まる (tsumaru): to be blocked, to be packed
