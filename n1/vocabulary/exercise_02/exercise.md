New words:


反応 (hannou): reaction, response


応用 (ouyou): application, put to practical use


適応 (tekiou): adaptation, accommodation


応答 (outou): reply, answer, response


なごやか: mild, calm, gentle


ゆるやか: loose, slack, gentle


おろそか: neglect, needless to say


しなやか: supple, flexible


対面 (taimen): interview, meeting


対比 (taihi): contrast, comparison


対処 (taisho): deal with, cope


対等 (taitou): equivalent


収集 (shuushuu): gathering up, collection, accumulation


収容 (shuuyou): accommondation, reception, housing


徴収 (choushuu): collection, levy


没収 (bosshuu): forfeiture, seizure, confiscation


話し合い (hanashiai): discussion, conference


健全 (kenzen): health, wholesome


寛容 (kan’you): forbearance, tolerance


精巧 (seikou): elaborate, delicate, exquisite


円満 (enman): perfection, harmony, peace


めど: aim, goal, prospect


きざし: signs, omen, symptoms


しかけ: device, trick, gadget


てがかり: clue, trail, track


選挙 (senkyo): election


投票 (touhyou): voting, poll


棄権 (kiken): abstaining from voting, renunciation of a right


欠席 (kesseki): absence, non-attendance


廃棄 (haiki): annulment, disposal


押し込む (oshikomu): to push into, to crowd into


乗り込む (norikomu): to board, to embark on


振り込む (furikomu): to step into, to break into, to raid


割り込む (warikomu): to cut in, to thrust oneself into


ラベル: label


レベル: level


ドリル: drill


ダブル: double
