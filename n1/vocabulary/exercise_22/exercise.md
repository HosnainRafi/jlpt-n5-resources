New words:


焦点 (shouten): focus, focal point


ほどける: to come untied, to come apart


ぼやける: to become dim, to become blurred


とぼける: to play dumb, to feign ignorance


ぼやく: to grumble, to complain


たっぷり: full, in plenty


わざわざ: expressly, especially, doing something especially rather than incidentally


じっくり: deliberately, carefully


つくづく: completely, really, thoroughly


乱暴 (ranbou): rude, violent


おどおど: coweringly, hesitantly


しみじみ: earnestly, keenly, fully


はらはら: exciting, thrilling


ぼつぼつ: gradually, here and there


平行 (heikou): side by side, concurrent


直線 (chokusen): straight line


うっとうしい: gloomy, depressing


こころぼそい: helpless, hopeless, disheartening


なさけない: miserable, pitiable, shameful


ものたりない: unsatisfied, unsatisfactory


再開発 (saikaihatsu): redevelopment


順調 (junchou): favorable, doing well


見合い (miai): formal marriage interview


見込み (mikomi): forecast, prospects


見積もり (mitsumori): estimation, quotation


見晴らし (miharashi): view, prospect, outlook


高速道路 (kousoku douro): highway, freeway


制限速度 (seigen sokudo): speed limit


違反 (ihan): violation (of law)


オーバー: over, exceeding


マーク: mark, mark up


アップ: up, upload


災害 (saigai): calamity, disaster


非公開 (hikoukai): private


無計画 (mukeikaku): lacking a plan


不平等 (fubyoudou): inequality, unfair


未開発 (mikaihatsu): undeveloped (countries), backward


わかわかしい: youthful, young


なれなれしい: over-familiar


非常時 (hijouji): emergency, crisis


切実 (setsujitsu): compelling, serious, severe


敏感 (binkan): sensibility, susceptibility


迅速 (jinsoku): quick, fast


頻繁 (hinpan): frequent, incessant
