New words:


無辜 (muko): innocent, blameless

裁決 (saiketsu): decision, ruling, judgement


所見 (shoken): view, opinion


審判 (shinban): refereeing, trial, judgement


処罰 (shobatsu): punishment


Ｘ線撮影 (ekkususen satsuei): x-ray photography


出血 (shukketsu): bleeding, haemorrhage


徴候 (choukou): sign, indication, symptom


措置 (sochi): measure, measures, step


解約 (kaiyaku): cancellation of contract


解放 (kaihou): release, unleashing, liberation


解釈 (kaishaku): explanation, interpretation


解除 (kaijo): cancellation, calling off


店舗 (tenpo): shop, store


識別 (shikibetsu): discrimination, discernment


指摘 (shiteki): pointing out, identification


認定 (nintei): authorization, acknowledgement


束縛 (sokubaku): restraint, restriction, binding


波打つ (namiutsu): to dash against, to wave, to undulate


凝らす (kogorasu): to freeze, to congeal, to concentrate one’s attention to


国鉄 (kokutetsu): national railway


搾取 (sakushu): exploitation, squeezing


収奪 (shuudatsu): plundering, exploitation


拓殖 (takushoku): colonization, exploitation


開発 (kaihatsu): development, exploitation


解禁 (kaikin): lifting a ban


反対論 (hantairon): opposing argument


堅調 (kenchou): bullish, firm (market)


強硬 (kyoukou): firm, vigorous, unyielding


健全 (kenzen): health, soundness


固体 (kotai): solid (body), solid matter


生かす (ikasu): to make (the best) use of


過多 (kata): excess, superabundance


過剰 (kajou): excess, over-


過労 (karou): overwork, strain


過密 (kamitsu): overcrowding, crowded


対策 (taisaku): counter-plan, counter-measure


気配 (kehai): indication, sign, hint


配慮 (hairyo): consideration, concern


配置 (haichi): arrangement (of resources), deployment


分配 (bunpai): division, sharing, distribution


部首 (bushu): radical (of a Kanji character)


予知 (yochi): foresight
