New words:


早めに (hayame ni): ahead of time


挑む (idomu): to challenge


取り組む (torikumu): to tackle, to deal with


心がける (kokorogakeru): to bear in mind, to aim to do


捗る (hakadoru): to make progress, to move right ahead (with the work)


目上 (meue): superior, senior


おろか: foolish, stupid


おろそか: neglect, carelessness


ぞんざい: rude, careless, slovenly


つきなみ: trite, common


主観 (shukan): subjectivity


悲観 (hikan): pessimism


予感 (yokan): premonition


直感 (chokkan): intuition, instict


常識 (joushiki): common knowledge, conventional wisdom


見識 (kenshiki): views, opinion, discernment


小説家 (shousetsuka): novelist, fiction writer


有益 (yuueki): beneficial, profitable


有望 (yuubou): good prospects, full of hope


もうれつ: violent, vehement, rage


ものずき: (idle) curiosity


だいたん: bold, daring, audacious


見通し (mitooshi): perspective, outlook


証明書 (shoumeisho): certificate, credentials


はなはだしい: extreme, excessive, intense


はげみ: (act of) encouragement, stimulus


はずみ: bounce, spring, momentum


ねばり: stickiness, viscosity


むすび: ending, conclusion, union
