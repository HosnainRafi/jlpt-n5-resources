New words:


洪水 (kouzui): flood


鉄道 (tetsudou): railroad


不通 (futsuu): suspension, interruption, stoppage


復旧 (fukkyuu): restoration, rehabilitation


良性 (ryousei): benign


良好 (ryoukou): favorable, satisfactory


良質 (ryoushitsu): good quality, superior quality


良識 (ryoushiki): good sense


合間 (aima): interval


手間 (tema): time, labor


空間 (kuukan): space, room


仲間 (nakama): fellow, comrade, associate


送料 (souryou): postage, carriage


一律 (ichiritsu): evenness, equality


一切 (issai): all, everything, entirely


おのずから: naturally, as a matter of course


かろうじて: barely, narrowly, just manage to do something


やむをえず: unavoidably, inevitably


高齢者 (koureisha): old person or people


年寄り (toshiyori): old people, the aged


介護 (kaigo): nursing


人材 (jinzai): man of talent, capable person


生育 (seiiku): growth, development


成熟 (seijuku): maturity, ripeness


養成 (yousei): training, development


祝日 (shukujitsu): national holidy


診る (miru): to examine (medically)


異常 (ijou): strangeness, abnormality


多忙 (tabou): busy, pressure of work


いやいや: reluctantly, unwillingly


おどおど: coweringly, hesitantly


ぐずぐず: slowly, hesitatingly


だらだら: sluggishly, endlessly


優勝 (yuushou): overall victory, championship


こみ上げる (komiageru): to fill (one’s heart), to feel nauseous


こぼれる: to spill, to overflow


大気汚染 (taikiosen): air pollution


対策 (taisaku): counter-plan, counter-measure


排気ガス (haikigasu): exhaust fumes


強化 (kyouka): strengthen, intensify


規制 (kisei): regulation, restriction


規格 (kikaku): standard, norm


規模 (kibo): scale, scope


規約 (kiyaku): agreement, protocal
