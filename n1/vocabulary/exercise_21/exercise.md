New words:


決議 (ketsugi): resolution, vote, decision


一致 (icchi): coincidence, agreement, union


採択 (saitaku): adoption, selection


満身 (manshin): the whole body


満場 (manjou): unanimous, whole audience


とうとい: precious, priceless


むなしく: in vain, to no purpose


ひさしく: for a long time


たやすい: easy, simple, light


救済 (kyuusai): relief, aid, rescue


養護 (yougo): nursing, (protective) care


介抱 (kaihou): nursing, looking after


奨励 (shourei): encouragement, promotion


拘束 (kousoku): restriction, restraint


制限 (seigen): restriction, limitation


束縛 (sokubaku): restraint, restriction


統制 (tousei): regulation, control


威力 (iryoku): power, authority, influence


迫力 (hakuryoku): force, intensity, strength


権威 (ken’i): authority, power, influence


権限 (kengen): power, jurisdiction, authority


国際交流 (kokusai kouryuu): international exchange


進路 (shinro): route, course


逃す (nogasu): to let loose, to set free


しのぐ: to outdo, to surpass, to endure


免れる (manugareru): to escape from, to be rescued from


避ける (yokeru): to avoid, to ward off, to avert
