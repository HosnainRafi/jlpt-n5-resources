New words:


信念 (shinnen): belief, faith, conviction


果たす (hatasu): to accomplish, to fulfill


打ち込む (uchikomu): to drive in, to devote onself to


やり遂げる (yaritogeru): to accomplish


貫く (tsuranuku): to go through


投票日 (touhyoubi): election day


当選 (tousen): being elected, winning


的確 (tekikaku): precise, accurate


確保 (kakuho): guarantee, ensure, secure


団体 (dantai): organization, association


国際交流 (kokusai kouryuu): international exchange


寄与 (kiyo): contribution, service


発足 (hossoku): starting, inauguration


発生 (hassei): outbreak, spring forth


発作 (hossa): fit, spasm


発進 (hasshin): departure, takeoff


ためる: to amass, to accumulate


たやす: to exterminate, to eradicate


たもつ: to keep, to preserve, to maintain


芽 (me): sprout


あこがれ: yearning, longing


朗らか (hogaraka): brightness, cheerfulness
