New words:


自然保護 (shizenhogo): nature conservation


開発計画 (kaihatsu keikaku): development project


促進 (sokushin): promotion, encouragement


考慮 (kouryo): consideration, taking into account


科学技術 (kagaku gijutsu): science and technology


画期的 (kakkiteki): ground-breaking, epoch-making


周期的 (shuukiteki): periodic, cyclic


定期的 (teikiteki): periodic


末期的 (makkiteki): decadent, terminal


はらはら: suspense, exciting, thrilling


ひらひら: flutter


ぶかぶか: too big, baggy


ふらふら: stagger, dizzy


きっかり: exactly, precisely


てっきり: surely, certainly, beyond doubt


じっくり: deliberately, carefully


きっちり: punctually, precisely


処置 (shochi): treatment, measure, step


救援 (kyuuen): relief, rescue, reinforcement


救助 (kyuujo): relief, aid, rescue


応急 (oukyuu): emergency, first-aid


応援 (ouen): aid, assistance, help


まぎらわしい: confusing, misleading


なやましい: seductive, troubling, hard


みすぼらしい: shabby, seedy


わずらわしい: troublesome, annoying


おおすじ: outline, summary


おもむき: meaning, tenor, appearance


作戦 (sakusen): tactics, strategy


練る (neru): to polish up (a plan), to work over


施す (hodokosu): to donate, to conduct, to apply


誂える (atsuraeru): to give an order, to place an order


差別 (sabetsu): discrimination, distinction


放送局 (housoukyoku): broadcasting office


協議 (kyougi): conference, discussion, negotiation


決議 (ketsugi): resolution, vote


物議 (butsugi): public discussion


抗議 (kougi): protest, objection
