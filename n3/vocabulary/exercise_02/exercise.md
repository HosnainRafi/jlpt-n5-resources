New words:


流れる (nagareru): to stream, to flow


沈む (shizumu): to sink, to feel depressed


浮く (uku): to float


犯人 (hannin): offender, criminal


逃げる (nigeru): to escape, to run away


見事 (migoto): splendid, magnificent


非常 (hijou): emergency, extraordinary, unusual


急激 (kyuugeki): sudden


必死 (hisshi): frantic, desperate


試合 (shiai): match, game, contest


勝つ (katsu): to win, to gain victory


引き (hiki): discount


比 (hi): ratio, proportion


差 (sa): difference, variation


別 (betsu): distinction, difference


計算 (keisan): calculation, forecast


重大 (juudai): serious, important, grave


複雑 (fukuzatsu): complex, complicated


正常 (seijou): normalcy, normality


問題解決 (mondai kaiketsu): problem-solving


積極的 (sekkyokuteki): positive


申込 (moushikomi): application, request, entry


関心 (kanshin): concern, interest


予想 (yosou): expectation, anticipation


手段 (shudan): means, way, measure


大地震 (daijishin): major earthquake


備え (sonae): preparation, provision


生放送 (namahousou): live broadcast


各 (kaku): each, every


数 (suu): several, a number of


効果 (kouka): effect, effectiveness, efficacy


影響 (eikyou): influence, effect


制限 (seigen): restriction, restraint


結果 (kekka): result, consequence, outcome


皮 (kawa): skin, pelt


折る (oru): to fold, to break


離す (hanasu): to part, to divide, to separate


剥く (muku): to peel, to skin


破る (yaburu): to tear, to smash


ラッシュアワー: rush hour


道路 (douro): road, highway


渋滞 (juutai): congestion, delay, stagnation


障害 (shougai): obstacle, handicap, hindrance


工事 (kouji): construction work


いまにも: at any time, soon


しばらく: little while, short while
