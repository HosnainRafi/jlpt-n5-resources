New words:


失敗 (shippai): failure, mistake, blunder


諦める (akirameru): to give up, to abandon


意志 (ishi): will, volition, determination


期待 (kitai): expectation, anticipation, hope


クイズ: quiz


望み (nozomi): wish, desire, hope


願い (negai): desire, wish, request


頼り (tayori): reliance, dependence​


寄り (yori): having a tendency towards


せっかく: with trouble, at great pains


もったいない: wasteful, impious, profane


とんでもない: unthinkable, outrageous


上品 (jouhin): elegant, refined, polished


派手 (hade): showy, flashy,


清潔 (seiketsu): clean, hygenic, sanitary


社長 (shachou): company president


地位 (chii): (social) position, status


田舎 (inaka): rural area, countryside


暮らし (kurashi): living, livelihood


集中 (shuuchuu): concentration


中身 (nakami): contents, interior


こぼれる (koboreru): to spill, to fall out of


ふた: cover, lid


しっかり: firmly, tightly


期待 (kitai): expectation, anticipation, hope
