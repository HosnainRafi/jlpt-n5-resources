New words:


資金 (shikin): funds, capital


安定した (antei shita): steady, stable, firm


経営 (keiei): management, administration


事業 (jigyou): enterprise, business, industry


拡大 (kakudai): magnification, enlargement, expansion


繋がる (tsunagaru): to be tied together, to be connected to


豊富 (houfu): abundance, wealthy, plenty


贅沢 (zeitaku): luxury, extravagance


幸福 (koufuku): happiness, blessedness


多量 (taryou): large quantity


真面目 (majime): serious, honest, earnest


ちゃっかり: shrewd, calculating, nervy


面 (men): face, mask, side


評判 (hyouban): fame, reputation, popularity


快適 (kaiteki): pleasant, comfortable


確認 (kakunin): affirmation, confirmation


冗談 (joudan): jest, joke


受話器 (juwaki): telephone receiver


大統領 (daitouryou): president


前進 (zenshin): drive, advance, progress


覚悟 (kakugo): resolution, readiness


感覚 (kankaku): sense, sensation, feeling


発覚 (hakkaku): detection, coming to light


自覚 (jikaku): self-consciousness, self-awareness


デザイン: design


工程 (koutei): manufacturing processs, work schedule


違い (chigai): difference, discrepancy


こだわる: to fuss over, to be particular about


造作 (zousa): trouble, difficulty


製造 (seizou): manufacture, production


創造 (souzou): creation


製品 (seihin): manufactured goods, product


単なる (tannaru): mere, simple, sheer


一致 (icchi): coincidence, consistency, match


自然 (shizen): naturally, spontaneously


天然 (tennen): nature, spontaneity


突然 (totsuzen): abrupt, sudden, unexpected


偶然 (guuzen): by chance, accident, unexpectedly


突き止める (tsukitomeru): to make sure, to ascertain, to locate
