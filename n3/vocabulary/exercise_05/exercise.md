New words:


昔 (mukashi): olden days, former


懐かしい (natsukashii): dear (old), beloved


羨ましい (urayamashii): jealous, envious


恥ずかしい (hazukashii): embarrassing, ashamed


調子 (choushi): condition, state of health


性質 (seishitsu): nature, disposition


態度 (taido): attitude, manner


身分 (mibun): social position, social status


部長 (buchou): head of a section or department


出席 (shusseki): to attend, to appear


出勤 (shukkin): going to work, reporting to work


出張 (shucchou): business trip


出国 (shukkoku): departure from a country


ぐらぐら (guragura): tottering, shaking


がらがら (garagara): with a clatter, with a rattle


ばらばら (barabara): scattered, disperse


ぶらぶら (burabura): dangling heavily


最近 (saikin): recently, lately


集める (atsumeru): to collect, to assemble


伝言 (dengon): verbal message, word (from someone)


応援 (ouen): assistance, help


注目 (chuumoku): notice, attention


世話 (sewa): looking after, help


絶対に (zettai ni): absolutely, unconditionally


多分 (tabun): perhaps, probably


必ず (kanarazu): certainly, without fail


ひどい (hidoi): cruel, heartless


怒る (okoru): to get angry, to get mad


当たり前 (atarimae): natural, reasonable


無駄 (muda): futility, waste


自由 (jiyuu): freedom


まし (mashi): better, preferable


当然 (touzen): natural, as a matter of course


気に入る (ki ni iru): to like, to be pleased with


曲 (kyoku): song, piece of music


人気 (ninki): popularity, public favor


覚える (oboeru): to remember


芝生 (shibafu): lawn


除く (nozoku): to remove


雑草 (zassou): weed


抜く (nuku): to pull out


楽しむ (tanoshimu): to enjoy (oneself)


防ぐ (fusegu): to defend oneself


育てる (sodateru): to raise, to bring up


衝突 (shoutotsu): collision, crash


ぶつかる (butsukaru): to bump into, to run into


止まる (tomaru): to stop, to come to a halt


壊れる (kowareru): to be broken, to break


動く (ugoku): to move, to stir
