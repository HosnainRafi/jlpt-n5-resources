New words:


通勤 (tsuukin): commuting to work


油 (abura): oil


気付く (kizuku): to notice, to recognize, to realize


切れる (kireru): to be out of, to expire


さっき: some time ago


ずっと前に (zutto mae ni): a long time ago


何回か (nankaika): a few times


何回も (nankaimo): time and time again


訳 (wake): reason, cause


流行 (ryuukou): fashion, craze, fad


流行る (hayaru): to be popular, to come into fashion


共通点 (kyoutsuuten): common feature


選手 (senshu): player (in game), team member


奪う (ubau): to snatch away, to dispossess


平気 (heiki): coolness, calmness, composure, unconcern


気にする (ki ni suru): to mind, to care about, to worry


欠点 (ketten): faults, defect, weakness


減る (heru): to decrease, to diminish
