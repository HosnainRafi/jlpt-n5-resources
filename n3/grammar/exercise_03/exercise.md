New words:


犠牲 (gisei): victim, sacrifice, scapegoat


破る (yaburu): to tear, to violate, to break, to destroy


鉄道 (tetsudou): railroad


列車 (ressha): train (ordinary)


関係 (kankei): relation, connection








大嵐 (ooarashi): raging storm


性格 (seikaku): character, personality


喧嘩 (kenka): quarrel, brawl, fight


数える (kazoeru): to count, to enumerate


分かれる (wakareru): to split, to separate, to divide


犯人 (hannin): offender, criminal


不規則 (fukisoku): irregularity, unsteadiness


妙 (myou): strange, unusual


目つき (metsuki): look, expression of the eyes


長所 (chousho): strong point, merit, virtue


短所 (tansho): defect, demerit, weak point, disadvantage


閉じこもる (tojikomoru): to seclude oneself, to shut oneself away


プロトタイプ: prototype


いじる: to touch, to tamper with, to grope about, to feel for something
