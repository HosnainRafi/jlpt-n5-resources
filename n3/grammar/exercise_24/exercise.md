New words:


あれやこれや: this and that


面倒 (mendou): trouble, difficulty, care


健康体 (kenkoutai): healthy body


前線 (zensen): front line








隠す (kakusu): to hide, to conceal


旧市街 (kyuushigai): the old town


向かう (mukau): to face, to go towards


答え (kotae): response, reply, answer


おそらく: perhaps, likely, probably


噴水 (funsui): water fountain


水音 (mizuoto): sound of water


信じる (shinjiru): to believe


呆然 (bouzen): dumbfounded, in blank amazement


見つめる (mitsumeru): to stare at, to gaze at


許可 (kyoka): permission, approval


見つかる (mitsukaru): to be found, to be discovered


コンサート: concert


手に入る (te ni hairu): to obtain, to come into possession of


見開く (mihiraku): to open one’s eyes


寝返りを打つ (negaeri o utsu): to turn over in one’s sleep
