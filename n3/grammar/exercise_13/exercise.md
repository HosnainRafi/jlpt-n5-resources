New words:


きっと: surely, undoubtedly


助ける (tasukeru): to help, to save, to rescue


表紙 (hyoushi): front cover, binding


演目 (enmoku): musical program


真夏 (manatsu): midsummer


夢 (yume): dream








上演 (jouen): performance, staging


人類 (jinrui): mankind, humanity


新た (arata): new, fresh, novel


地球 (chikyuu): the earth


惑星 (wakusei): planet


加える (kuwaeru): to add, to append


繰り返す (kurikaesu): to repeat, to do something over again


現状 (genjou): present condition, existing state


人口 (jinkou): population


人間 (ningen): human being, man


増える (fueru): to increase, to multiply


多く (ooku): many, much, largely, abundantly


賞金 (shoukin): prize, monetary award


著しく: considerably; remarkably


得る (eru): to get, to acquire, to obtain


妻 (tsuma): wife


同志 (doushi): comrade, same mind


横になる (yoko ni naru): to lie down


つぶる: to close the eyes


気を静める (ki o shizumeru): to becalm one’s feelings, to compose oneself


息 (iki): breath, breathing


こっそり: stealthily, secretly


抜け出す (nukedasu): to slip out, to sneak away


ビジネスマン: businessman


頭文字 (kashiramoji): initials, first letter of a word


並ぶ (narabu): to line up


略語 (ryakugo): abbreviation


ハードウエア: hardware


ソフトウエア: software


専門家 (senmonka): specialist


一般 (ippan): general, universal


販売 (hanbai): sales, selling, marketing
