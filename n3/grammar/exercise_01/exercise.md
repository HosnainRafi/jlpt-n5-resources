New words:


嫌 (iya): disagreeable, detestable


学費 (gakuhi): tuition, school expenses


払う (harau): to pay


講義 (kougi): lecture


受ける (ukeru): to receive, to accept


特質 (tokushitsu): characteristic, feature, special quality


うらやましい: envious, jealous, enviable


現実 (genjitsu): reality


直面 (chokumen): confrontation


いとう: to dislike, to hate, to grudge (doing)


逃げる (nigeru): to escape, to run away


資源 (shigen): resources


争い (arasoi): dispute, quarrel


両国 (ryoukoku): both countries


関係 (kankei): relation, connection


ますます: increasingly, more and more


悪化 (akka): deterioration, growing worse, corruption


未明 (mimei): early dawn, gray of morning








関東 (kantou): Kantou (eastern half of Japan)


地域 (chiiki): area, region


範囲 (han’i): extent, scope


対応 (taiou): interaction, correspondence, dealing with


父親 (chichioya): father


想像 (souzou): imagination, guess


きちんと: precisely, accurately


向き合う (mukiau): to be opposite, to face each other


実家 (jikka): (one’s parents) home


パン屋 (pan’ya): bakery


フェンス: fence


飛び越える (tobikoeru): to jump over, to clear


老け込む (fukekomu): to age, to become old


卒業 (sotsugyou): graduation, completion (of a course)


不敬 (fukei): disrespect
