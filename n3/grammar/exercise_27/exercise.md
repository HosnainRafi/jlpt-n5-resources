New words:


酔う (you): to get drunk, to become intoxicated


殴る (naguru): to strike, to hit


意見 (iken): opinion, view


むしろ: rather, instead


質問 (shitsumon): question, inquiry


証拠 (shouko): evidence, proof


見つめる (mitsumeru): to stare at, to gaze at


マフラー: muffler, scarf


編む (amu): to knit, to braid


毛糸 (keito): knitting wool


一体 (ittai): the heck…, in the world…


最高 (saikou): highest, supreme


専門家 (senmonka): specialist


説明 (setsumei): explanation


変 (hen): strange, odd








遠慮 (enryo): diffidence, restraint, reserve


恥ずかしがり屋 (hazukashigariya): shy person


やる気 (yaruki): willingness, eagerness


天然資源 (tennenshigen): natural resources


減る (heru): to decrease


需要 (juyou): demand, request


急増 (kyuuzou): explosion, surge


困難 (konnan): difficulty, distress


清浄 (seijou): pure, clean, purity


入手 (nyuushu): obtaining, coming to hand


視界 (shikai): field of vision


ぼやける: to become dim, to become blurred


頭痛 (zutsuu): headache


耐える (taeru): to bear, to stand, to put up with
