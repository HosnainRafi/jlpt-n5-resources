New words:


二度と (nido to): never again (with negative verb)


約束 (yakusoku): promise, appointment


読み返す (yomikaesu): to reread, to read again


たまらない: intolerable, unbearable


哀しい (kanashii): sad, sorrowful


気持 (kimochi): feeling, sensation, mood


経つ (tatsu): to pass, to elapse


そろそろ: soon


だんだん gradually


ようやく: finally, at last


いつの間にか (itsunomanika): before one knows, before one becomes aware of


死 (shi): death








分かつ (wakatsu): to divide, to separate


幸せ (shiawase): happiness, good fortune, luck


結婚生活 (kekkon seikatsu): married life


送る (okuru): to send, to spend (time)


蛇口 (jaguchi): faucet, tap


傷つく (kizutsuku): to be wounded, to be injured


前腕 (maeude): forearm


氷 (koori): ice


導く (michibiku): to guide, to lead, to show the way


まさか: by no means


鍵 (kagi): key, lock


お客様 (okyakusama): guest, visitor, customer


内密 (naimitsu): privacy, secrecy


程度 (teido): degree, amount


商品 (shouhin): commodity, goods, merchandise


腰 (koshi): back, lower back, hips


痛み (itami): pain, ache, sore


効果 (kouka): effect, effectiveness, result


詳しく (kuwashiku): in detail, fully, at length
