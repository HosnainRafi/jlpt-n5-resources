出発 (shuppatsu): departure

濡れる (nureru): to get wet


急に (kyuu ni): rapidly; suddenly; abruptly


構いません (kamaimasen): no problem; it doesn’t matter

お持ち帰り (omochikaeri): takeout (i.e. food); takeaway
